Share a screen or tab. When something on it changes, a decision model (Liquid's d1-ultra by default, or d1) decides whether a blind user should hear about it, and this page speaks what it decided.

Privacy: what leaves this machine

Every frame pair this page judges is sent to Liquid's cloud, and to Claude when narration is on. Share only a window or tab with nothing private on it: no mail, chats, passwords, banking or documents. The demo targets below are safe to share.
